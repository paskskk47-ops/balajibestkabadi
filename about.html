Our Story & Mission

Transforming Jaipur's local scrap trade into a professional, digitized, eco-friendly recycling system.

The Metamorphosis: Traditional Trade Meets Eco-Tech

For decades, the scrap business in Jaipur was stuck in the past. Residents calling a local kabadi wala in Jaipur faced the same recurring problems: manual weighing scales that were frequently manipulated, highly opaque daily pricing, erratic pickup schedules, and professional unreliability. Scrap collection wasn't viewed as a service; it was viewed as a chore fraught with skepticism.

"We saw a structural problem where others saw junk. The traditional scrap trade lacked trust, accuracy, and ecological vision. We set out to change that forever."

Founded in Jaipur, Rajasthan, Balaji Kabadi Wala was born out of a singular, powerful vision by our founder. Having witnessed the clear lack of transparency and scale inaccuracy in traditional doorstep collection, we wanted to elevate the profession. We set out to build a platform where scrap collection feels like a modern tech-enabled startup service rather than a local junk transaction.

We introduced calibrated, highly precise electronic digital weighing scales that eliminate weight manipulation entirely. We decided to publish live daily market scrap rates online, ensuring absolute transparency. Finally, we removed the hassle of cash-only systems by introducing instant payments on the spot via UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm) and bank transfers.

Today, Balaji Kabadi Wala is more than just a doorstep service. We are an eco-conservation movement. Under our "Clean India, Green Jaipur" pledge, we systematically sort every kilogram of newspaper, plastic, metal, cardboard, and electronic waste (e-waste) we collect. Instead of letting waste rot in local landfills, we route materials directly to licensed, eco-certified recycling plants across Rajasthan.
