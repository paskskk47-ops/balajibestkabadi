Unlocking Value from Copper & Brass: Household Scrap Identification Guide

👤 Balaji Kabadi Wala 📅 May 23, 2025 ⏱️ 7 min read 📍 Jaipur, Rajasthan

If you look around your storerooms, kitchen cabinets, or electrical conduits, you might find valuable metals that can earn you premium payouts. Among all the items you can sell to a scrap dealer, copper and brass are by far the most valuable. While newspaper sells for ₹18/kg and iron for ₹32/kg, copper wire can fetch you well over ₹1150/kg!

However, many people make the mistake of selling mixed copper and brass at lower alloy prices. In this guide, we will help you identify copper vs brass, understand wire grading, and get the highest scrap rates in Jaipur today.

How to Tell the Difference: Copper vs. Brass

It is easy to confuse these metals if they are dusty or tarnished. Here is how to distinguish them:

  • Color: Pure copper has a distinct reddish-brown color (like a new coin). Brass is an alloy of copper and zinc, giving it a bright yellowish-gold color (like jewelry or vintage keys).
  • Sound Test: Tap the metal with a steel rod. Copper produces a dull, short sound. Brass is highly resonant and will produce a clear, ringing chime (which is why bells and cymbals are made of brass).
  • Hardness: Copper is a softer, flexible metal that can be bent easily. Brass is extremely hard and rigid.

Understanding Copper Scrap Grades

All copper is not valued equally. We categorize copper scrap into three main grades:

  1. Grade 1 (Bare Bright Copper): This is the highest-paying grade. It consists of clean, unalloyed, uncoated copper wires or bars that have been stripped of plastic insulation. It must be free of paint, solder, or corrosion. Rate: ₹1150 / Kg.
  2. Grade 2 (Insulated Wire Copper): This is copper wire with its plastic jacket still intact. Since we must strip or process the wire to retrieve the copper, the rate is lower. Rate: ₹400 – ₹550 / Kg depending on wire thickness.
  3. Brass (Peetal): Yellow brass from plumbing fittings, valves, water meters, and old showpieces is valued at ₹750 / Kg.
