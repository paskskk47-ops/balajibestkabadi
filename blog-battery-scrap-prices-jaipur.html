Lead-Acid Battery Scrap Price in Jaipur | Sell Old Car Battery for Cash

👤 Balaji Kabadi Wala 📅 May 25, 2025 ⏱️ 5 min read 📍 Jaipur, Rajasthan

Whether you own a car, a motorcycle, or an inverter backup system at home, you will eventually have to replace its heavy lead-acid battery. But once a battery goes dead, what do you do with it? Many homeowners leave dead batteries in their storerooms or garages, unaware that they are sitting on a valuable piece of scrap that can easily fetch them hundreds of rupees.

At Balaji Kabadi Wala, we buy all types of old batteries, including automotive batteries, motorcycle batteries, and heavy-duty tubular inverter batteries. In this guide, we will cover the current battery scrap rates in Jaipur and explain how to dispose of them safely while earning maximum payouts.

⚠️ Safety Warning: Lead-acid batteries contain corrosive sulfuric acid and heavy lead plates. Never attempt to break open a battery or pour the acid out on open ground. Keep dead batteries out of reach of children and pets, and always sell them to government-licensed recyclers.

How Lead-Acid Battery Scrap Rates are Calculated

Unlike regular scrap metal or cardboard which is valued strictly per kilogram, batteries are valued based on two different systems depending on the customer's preference:

  1. Weight-Based Rate (Per Kilogram): Under this system, the battery is weighed on our digital scale. The current lead-acid battery rate in Jaipur averages ₹80 – ₹90 per Kilogram. This is highly recommended for heavy commercial or solar system batteries.
  2. Unit-Based Flat Rate (Per Piece): For standard vehicle batteries, we offer flat rates based on the Ampere-Hour (AH) capacity:
    • 🏍️ Motorcycle Battery (3AH - 9AH): ₹80 – ₹150 / piece
    • 🚗 Small Hatchback Car Battery (32AH - 45AH): ₹500 – ₹700 / piece
    • 🚘 SUV & Sedan Car Battery (65AH - 80AH): ₹800 – ₹1100 / piece
    • 🔋 Tubular Inverter Battery (100AH - 150AH): ₹1800 – ₹2500 / piece
    • ⚡ Heavy Truck Battery (120AH - 180AH): ₹3000 – ₹4500 / piece

Why Battery Recycling is Extremely Important

Lead-acid batteries are highly hazardous if disposed of incorrectly. Over 98% of a lead-acid battery is recyclable. The lead plates are melted down and purified, the plastic casing is ground down to make new cases, and the sulfuric acid is neutralized or processed for industrial chemical use. By recycling your battery with us, you ensure that toxic lead does not pollute the soil and groundwater of Rajasthan.
