Newspaper Raddi Price in Jaipur — Get Maximum Value for Old Papers

👤 Balaji Kabadi Wala 📅 May 17, 2025 ⏱️ 5 min read 📍 Jaipur, Rajasthan

Every Jaipur household accumulates old newspapers, books, and cardboard every month. Instead of letting them pile up, sell them to a trusted kabadi wala and earn instant cash. This guide covers current raddi prices in Jaipur and exactly how to maximise your earnings.

📰 Current Raddi Rate Jaipur (May 2025): Old Newspapers — ₹17–18/kg | Books/Magazines — ₹6–10/kg | Cardboard Boxes — ₹13/kg

Why Sell Raddi Regularly?

An average Jaipur family generates 10–20 kg of newspaper monthly. At ₹17/kg, that's ₹170–₹340 every month — over ₹3,000 per year — sitting unused in your storeroom. Selling regularly also reduces household clutter and supports paper recycling mills across Rajasthan.

Tips to Maximise Your Raddi Value

🔍 Sort By Type

Keep newspapers separate from books and cardboard. Mixed paper always gets the lowest rate.

☀️ Keep It Dry

Wet paper weighs more but fetches less. Always store paper in a dry, covered area before calling.

📦 Bundle Neatly

Tie newspapers in neat stacks with jute rope. This speeds up collection and signals quality.

⚖️ Verify Weight

Use your kitchen scale to estimate weight before the kabadi wala arrives to cross-check.

What Paper Items Can You Sell?

  • Daily Hindi & English newspapers (Dainik Bhaskar, Rajasthan Patrika, TOI)
  • Old textbooks, school books, college notes
  • Magazines (India Today, Grihshobha, etc.)
  • Office paper waste and printouts
  • Cardboard boxes, Amazon/Flipkart packaging cartons
  • Old diaries, notebooks, registers
